NusaData — Indonesia in numbers. An original, independent presentation of public statistics published by Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) and the Ministry of Health. Not an official BPS website. Figures carry their source and year on every page; the Method & sources page lists each publication.

Static site: no accounts, no tracking, no network requests. Works offline once loaded.
